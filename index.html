How to swap Polygon in 4 steps

Start with native POL on Polygon PoS and a receiving address for your chosen asset. Keep a little POL aside for the network fee.

Start your Polygon swap ↗
  1. 01

    Choose your pair and networks

    Select POL on Polygon PoS as your input. Choose USDC, ETH or USDT and the receiving network. POL on Ethereum is a separate route from native POL on Polygon.

  2. 02

    Review the full quote

    Enter your amount. Check the minimum, service fee or spread, destination charges and quote expiry. Compare the final amount you receive, then check your receiving address.

  3. 03

    Send or confirm the transaction

    Follow the route’s displayed instructions. For a deposit-based swap, send the exact requested amount to the order address on Polygon PoS. For a wallet-executed swap, review and sign the transaction in your wallet.

  4. 04

    Follow your asset to its destination

    For a deposit-based route, the service releases the asset after your Polygon transaction confirms and order processing completes. Track the payout on the destination network using its transaction hash.

How much does it cost to swap Polygon?

Polygon PoS gas is typically very low, and the native fee token is POL. That network fee is just one part of your total swap cost.

Polygon PoS is an EVM-compatible chain anchored to Ethereum. The broader Ethereum scaling and L2 ecosystem also includes Polygon CDK and AggLayer, which connects sovereign chains. Select the actual network shown in your wallet.

01 / Network gas

Paid to execute your Polygon transaction. Keep native POL available; swapping your entire balance leaves no room for gas.

02 / Service fee & spread

A provider can charge a separate fee or include its margin in the exchange rate. Judge a quote by the output amount after these costs.

03 / Destination costs

Cross-chain routes and exchange withdrawals add their own costs. Low Polygon gas does not determine the fee for receiving ETH on Ethereum.

A note on rate timing. POL prices and route liquidity move continuously. Refresh a quote just before sending. A floating rate follows the service’s execution terms; a fixed rate applies only within its stated time and deposit conditions. For an onchain route, also review price impact, slippage tolerance and minimum received.

Cannot swap POL? Check gas, approval and liquidity

Start with the network, the gas balance and the transaction status.

“Insufficient funds for gas”

Leave native POL in your Polygon wallet. Reduce the swap amount or add POL on Polygon PoS. An ERC-20 POL balance on Ethereum cannot pay Polygon gas.

The wallet still says MATIC

Update your wallet and network token label. Native MATIC on Polygon PoS upgraded automatically in 2024. For MATIC on Ethereum, use the migration instructions linked in the official upgrade notice above.

The quote expired or the swap reverted

Get a fresh quote and check the minimum amount, liquidity and slippage settings. A reverted onchain transaction still consumes gas. Check its status before submitting another transaction.

Payment sent, output not visible

Check PolygonScan first. If the input succeeded, review the order status and payout hash. Switch your wallet to the receiving network and verify the token contract. For a wrong-network deposit, contact the service with your order ID and hash.

Before you swap Polygon: amounts, networks and gas

Quick answers before your next swap.

How do I swap Polygon to USDC?

Select POL on Polygon PoS as the asset you pay and USDC as the asset you receive. Choose the receiving network, enter your amount and receiving address, then review the quote. Follow the displayed transaction instructions and keep enough POL in your wallet for gas.

Do I swap Polygon using POL or MATIC?

Use POL for the native token on Polygon PoS. MATIC on Polygon PoS upgraded automatically to POL on September 4, 2024. An old wallet label can still say MATIC; update the wallet or network token symbol. MATIC held on Ethereum has a separate migration process.

What are Polygon swap fees?

There are separate cost layers: the Polygon network gas fee, the service fee or spread in the swap quote, and any destination-network or withdrawal fee. Polygon PoS gas is typically very low and paid in POL. Compare the final amount received for the same input and destination network.

Can I swap Polygon to ETH on Ethereum?

Yes, through a route that explicitly supports POL on Polygon PoS to ETH on Ethereum. Select Ethereum as the receiving network. WETH received on Polygon stays on Polygon; it is not native ETH on Ethereum. Check both the token and network before confirming.

How long does a Polygon swap take?

Timing includes Polygon transaction confirmation, service processing and confirmation on the receiving network. Polygon confirmations are fast, but cross-chain delivery follows the full route. Use the order status and transaction hashes to track each stage.

Why does my Polygon swap show insufficient gas?

Your wallet needs native POL on Polygon PoS to pay network gas. Reduce the amount you are swapping or add POL on the same network. USDC, USDT and WETH do not replace the native gas balance for a standard wallet transaction.
